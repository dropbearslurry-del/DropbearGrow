Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026  ·  Last updated: 8 April 2026

Dropbear Slurry Pty Ltd (ACN 694 072 952, ABN 84 694 072 952) ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and the associated Square Online storefront. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily provide it — for example when you place an order, contact us, or sign up for our newsletter. This may include:

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number, delivery address)
  • Payment information — processed securely by Square; we do not store card details
  • Messages and enquiries you send us
  • Basic usage data collected automatically by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and Square, such as IP address and browser type

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders and send shipping notifications
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Send newsletters or product updates where you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve our website and product offering
  • Meet our legal and tax obligations as an Australian business

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website and store rely on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Square — payment processing and online store. See Square's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — static website hosting. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts — font delivery. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Australia Post / courier partners — delivery of physical orders.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This website (dropbearslurry-del.github.io) does not use cookies or analytics tracking beyond what GitHub Pages and Google Fonts collect by default as part of serving the page. Our Square storefront may use cookies for cart and session management — see Square's cookie policy for details.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored on secure servers operated by Square and GitHub. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access. However, no data transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by Australian law (for example, transaction records for tax purposes are kept for a minimum of 5 years).

7. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or out-of-date information
  • Complain about a breach of the APPs

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below. We will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
