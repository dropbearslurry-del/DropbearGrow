Application

Four Steps to Fed Plants

01

Measure

Add 200mL of concentrate to your watering can or mixing vessel.

02

Dilute

Top up to 10L with tap water. Shake or stir gently to combine.

03

Apply

Water directly to soil at the base of plants. Works as a foliar spray too.

04

Watch

Most growers see visible improvement within 5–7 days. Repeat weekly or fortnightly.

About Us

Built for Growers,
Not Shelves

Dropbear Slurry started with a simple idea: growers deserve access to the same commercial-grade nutrition that professionals use — without the industrial minimums or the marketing fluff.

K-666 and K-333 are concentrated liquid fertilisers formulated in Queensland with a focus on fast uptake, pH balance, and plant safety. K-666 is built for established plants and heavy feeders. K-333 is our gentler formula — lower potassium silicate, ideal for cuttings, seedlings, and sensitive species.
