Application Guide

5 Tips for Better Results

1

Start with K-333

Always begin seedlings and new cuttings on the gentler K-333 formula before graduating to K-666.

2

Dilute correctly

Never apply at full concentration. Start at 1:500 and adjust based on plant response over 2–3 waterings.

3

Water first

Apply diluted tonic to pre-moistened soil. Reduces root shock and improves uptake efficiency.

4

Foliar feeding

A 1:500 spray direct to leaves accelerates uptake — great for fast-growing crops and deficiency correction.

5

Switch at flowering

Transition to K-666 once plants enter the flowering stage to maximise bud set and fruiting.
